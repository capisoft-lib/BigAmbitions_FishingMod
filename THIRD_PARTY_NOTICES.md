# Third-party audio notices

FishingMod redistributes eight modified sound effects. Every source below is released under
[Creative Commons Zero 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), which permits
copying, modification and redistribution without attribution. Credits are retained here so the
origin of every packaged file remains auditable.

| Packaged file | Source and author | Processing for FishingMod |
|---|---|---|
| `Sounds/cast-whoosh.wav` | [Casting Fishing Rod for Game Fishing SFX](https://freesound.org/people/el_boss/sounds/853287/) by **el_boss** (CC0; itself assembled from CC0 material) | HQ preview converted from MP3, folded to mono, filtered, faded and encoded as 44.1 kHz PCM 16-bit WAV. |
| `Sounds/bobber-splash.wav` | `fishing-plop.ogg`, prepared by [OpenMMO](https://github.com/Julian-adv/OpenMMO/blob/master/doc/assets/sfx.md) from `bubble_02` in [40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx) by **rubberduck** | Level adjusted, tail faded, resampled and encoded as mono PCM WAV. |
| `Sounds/reel-out.wav` and `Sounds/reel-in.wav` | `fishing-reel.ogg`, prepared by [OpenMMO](https://github.com/Julian-adv/OpenMMO/blob/master/doc/assets/sfx.md) from `click_004` in [Kenney Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) (CC0) | Ratchet repeated, filtered and pitch/time adjusted into separate outgoing and incoming reel variants; encoded as mono PCM WAV. |
| `Sounds/qte-success.wav` and `Sounds/qte-failure.wav` | `ui-confirm.wav` and `ui-error.wav` from [Arcade Interface SFX](https://colorosse.com/assets/audio/sfx/arcade-ui-sfx) by **Colorosse** (CC0) | Level reduced for frequent QTE feedback; retained as 44.1 kHz mono PCM 16-bit WAV. |
| `Sounds/fish-landed.wav` | `fishing-splash.ogg` and `fishing-catch.ogg`, prepared by [OpenMMO](https://github.com/Julian-adv/OpenMMO/blob/master/doc/assets/sfx.md) from `splash_03` by **rubberduck** and `jingles_PIZZI06` from [Kenney Music Jingles](https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles) (both CC0) | Splash and short success accent mixed, limited, faded and encoded as mono PCM WAV. |
| `Sounds/line-snap.wav` | `fishing-snap.ogg`, prepared by [OpenMMO](https://github.com/Julian-adv/OpenMMO/blob/master/doc/assets/sfx.md) from `pluck_001` in [Kenney Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) (CC0) | Level adjusted and encoded as 44.1 kHz mono PCM 16-bit WAV. |
